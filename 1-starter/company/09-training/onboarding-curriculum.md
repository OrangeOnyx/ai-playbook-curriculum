# Onboarding Curriculum — New Employee AI Training

Every new employee completes this within their first 30 days. Structured as five 45-minute modules.

## Module 1 — What AI can and can't do here (Day 1)

**Objectives:**
- Understand what tools we've approved
- Know what the tools do well and what they don't
- Know our data classification rules

**Content:**
- Tour of the approved AI tools (chat, IDE, meeting assistant, department copilot)
- The 4-tier data classification: Public / Internal / Confidential / Restricted
- The tool tiers: which tools take which classes
- Live demo: routing an example task to the right tool

**Assessment:**
- 10-question quiz on data classification
- Must pass 90% to continue

## Module 2 — Your first workflow (Week 1)

**Objectives:**
- Compose a good prompt
- Understand system prompts vs user prompts
- Use RAG (department copilot) effectively

**Content:**
- The 4-part prompt: Role, Task, Constraints, Format
- Live-write a prompt for a real task with the trainer
- How to use the department copilot: when to search KB vs when to reason
- How to iterate: what to do when the answer is wrong

**Assessment:**
- Complete 3 real tasks with AI assistance
- Submit prompts + outputs to trainer for review

## Module 3 — Personal setup (Week 1)

**Objectives:**
- Configure your master system prompt
- Load the About Me block
- Set memory hygiene defaults

**Content:**
- Work through `individual/03-system-prompts/` templates
- Configure ChatGPT Custom Instructions
- Configure Claude Project settings
- Configure Perplexity Spaces
- Configure Cursor / Copilot personal preferences

**Assessment:**
- Show your master system prompt to the trainer
- Show your Custom Instructions
- Demonstrate memory is set correctly

## Module 4 — Guardrails and safety (Week 2)

**Objectives:**
- Know what NOT to paste into AI
- Know how to spot a hallucination
- Know how to report incidents

**Content:**
- Data classification examples — role-play "would you paste this?"
- Hallucination examples: fabricated citations, wrong facts, made-up features
- Verification patterns: cross-check, ask for sources, use grounded tools
- Incident reporting: what, when, how, to whom

**Assessment:**
- Complete 5 "spot the problem" case studies
- Discuss with trainer

## Module 5 — Your department's playbook (Week 3)

**Objectives:**
- Deep dive on your department's copilot
- Learn your department's top 5 AI workflows
- Meet your AI Champion

**Content:**
- Facilitated by department AI Champion
- Walk through the department copilot's prompt library
- Practice with 3 real recent tasks
- Q&A

**Assessment:**
- Ship 1 real work output using AI in your first week post-training
- Peer or manager reviews the output

## Continuing education

After the 5 modules:
- Weekly "AI office hours" (30 min, opt-in)
- Monthly "what shipped" showcase across the company
- Quarterly deep-dive on a specific topic
- Certifications for advanced users (see quarterly-refresh)

- Slide deck (in company Drive)
- Recordings of past sessions
- FAQ document
- Exercise workbook
- Assessment answer keys
