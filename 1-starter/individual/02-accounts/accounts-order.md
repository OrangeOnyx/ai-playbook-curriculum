## Rule of thumb

If you're spending more than $150/mo on subscriptions, one of them is unused. Audit quarterly.

## Enterprise upgrades

If you're using any of these for work with sensitive data, upgrade to the Enterprise / Team / Business tier for that provider. The delta buys you:
- SSO / SCIM
- No training on your data (with contract)
- Admin console + audit logs
- DPA / BAA availability
- Increased context or rate limits
